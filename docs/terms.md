# Terms for the menus

Proposed English for the names and labels that recur across the town and castle
menus (STATUS.OBJ and SISETU.OBJ). Once agreed, every string uses these.

Where the game already carries its own English, that wins: classes, races,
alignments, stats, ailments and spell names all come from the built-in lists in
STATUS.OBJ (102-221, 222-305, 625-635). Past that, the long-standing English names
from the Wizardry series are used where Dimguil's Japanese matches them, since
players know them. Otherwise it's translated from the Japanese. Marked **(choice)**
where the series' English and a literal reading differ.

## Combat commands

Fight (たたかう), Parry (みをまもる), Spell (じゅもん), Dispel (のろいをとく),
Use (アイテム), Run (にげる), Ambush (きしゅう), Hide (かくれる), Breath (ブレス).
These are the series' commands. アイテム is literally "Item"; **(choice)** "Use" is
what the series shows.

## Item categories

The catalog (ITEM.DAT, already ours) uses short forms for its narrow column: Weapons,
Armor, Shields, Helms, Gloves, Boots, Music, Other; Swords, Katanas, Wands, Axes, Maces,
Staves, Spears, Bows, Thrown; Leather, Robes, Chain, Breast, Plate, Hats; Potions,
Scrolls, Cloaks, Ankhs, Rings, Amulets, Mystic. Boltac's shop lists the same
categories with more room, so it gets the full names where they fit: Wands & Daggers,
Axes & Hammers, Maces & Whips, Spears & Polearms, Thrown Weapons, Leather Armor, Chain
Mail, Breastplates, Plate Mail, Instruments, Mystic Items.

## The temple's chant

祈り- ささやき- 詠唱- 念じろ! is the series' famous resurrection chant. In the Japanese
games it runs pray, whisper, chant, invoke; the English games had it as "Murmur- Chant-
Pray- Invoke!". **(choice)** The English games' version, which players will recognise,
or a literal one in the Japanese order.
