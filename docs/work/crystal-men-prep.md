## The Crystal Man

- Enchanted type (魔法生物系), level 24, HP 168-264 each, AC -30. 3-6 appear; the
  bot met 5.
- Attack A: eye beams, 5-17 damage, reaches the back row, 25% decapitation.
- Attack B: hand rays, 11-35 damage, reaches the back row, 40% paralysis.
- Spells: Labadi, Mogato.
- Resists: Mage 30%, Priest 70%, Alchemist 30%, Psionic 70%; fire, cold,
  non-elemental, sleep, stone, insanity, paralysis, KO, decapitation. No weakness.

## Spells

- Cortu (Mage 3): a barrier against enemy spells and breath, stronger with the
  caster's level; stacks. Against Labadi. Venus and Eve have Mage level 3 slots.
- Bamatu (Priest 3, party AC -3, stacks), Maporfic (Priest 4, party AC -2), Porfic
  (Priest 1, caster AC -4), Sogref (Mage 2, one ally AC -2): a lower AC means fewer
  beam and ray hits, and decapitation and paralysis only come with a hit.
- Dialko (Priest 3) cures paralysis; Madi (Priest 6) cures everything short of death.
- Morfis (Alchemist 5) cures the whole party's conditions except stone, death, ash.

## What the chapter 16 party carries

None of the items above. Useful things it does have: Earth's STAFF OF CURING (uses
Morfis: cures the party's paralysis at once), four POTIONS OF DIAL (Dialko) on Earth
and Mercury, SHADOW CLOAKs (Sogref) on Uranus, Venus and Earth, and Mercury's SCROLL
OF MAPOBA (party AC -2, Crystal Men AC +2). Earth has all seven Priest levels; Venus
and Eve have Mage levels 1-5.

## Weaknesses and debuffs (added after Chris's buff/debuff question)

The site lists no weakness (弱点：なし). Mogato only kills a demon-type target, so
against the party it always does nothing; Labadi (leaves one character 1-8 HP) is
their only spell that matters.

Can't work: sleep (Katino, Katiqurea), paralysis (Lorks), stone, confusion (Sios,
Lios, Masios, Lasios; the WAND OF MYSTERY's Masios), instant death (Badi, Bakadi,
the GOLD ARMOR's Badi), decapitation. Fire, cold and non-elemental damage is
resisted.

Not on their resist list, so worth trying:
- Morlis (Mage 3): fear on the whole group, which stops them acting, and AC +4.
  Mage school, so about 30% nullified.
- Dilto (Mage 2): group AC +2, never nullified, stacks.
- Mapoba (Mercury's scroll) and Poba: their AC up, the party's down.
- Bacortu (Mage 5): blocks the group's spells (Labadi). Mage school, ~30% nullified.
- Montino (Priest 2): silence, but Priest school, ~70% nullified.
- Single-target damage with no element: Tzalik (Mage 4, 24-58; Eve's did 35).
- Badumas (Psionic 4) lowers their attack power, but nobody in the party is a Psionic.

No haste exists for this party: Ponti (more attacks) is Alchemist only.
