## How claims are marked

Every claim in the chapters carries how it was established:

- **[code]**: read in the decompiled C, or in the disassembly where the C was unclear
  (the chapters say which).
- **[emu]**: seen in the emulator (`scripts/emu.py`), with how the case was set up. Most
  tests used button presses and save states only; where a test set its case up by
  writing to RAM, the chapter says so.
- **[ram]**: read from a RAM dump of real play. **[dump]**: from running the game's own
  code over a RAM dump (`scripts/re/talkscripts.py`). **[record]**: read from a data
  record on the disc.
- **[inf]** or "inferred": not confirmed.

The symbol files (`scripts/re/symbols/*.csv`) mark each name `confirmed` (with how) or
`inferred` the same way. Where two chapters disagreed, the later reading was checked
against the C and the earlier text corrected in place, saying so.

## Tools

The workflow is in `code-map.md`, "How to work". In short: read
`rips/re/decomp/<MOD>/<addr>_<name>.c`, name things in your CSV, run
`python3 scripts/re/ghidra.py refresh <MOD>`. The other tools in `scripts/re/`:

- `talkscripts.py` and `talklines.py`: dump the talk scripts and map every NPC line
- `recdump.py`: print roster, party, item and monster records with their fields named
- `fielduse.py`: which functions use which field of a typed global
- `savefile.py`: decode a memory card save
- `desccheck.py`: compare descriptions with item and spell records
- `strmap.py`: which function prints which SISETU or STATUS string
- `patchsites.py`: where our `asm/` patches change the game's code
- `cfold.py`: fold the talk handlers' repeated loops for reading
- `battle_checks/`: the emulator scripts behind D's checks

## Rules at a glance

The formulas a player would want, each with where it was read. rand(n) below is 0..n-1
unless it says inclusive; `rand_upto(n)` is 0..n inclusive.

### Encounters ([A-maze.md §7-8](A-maze.md#7-encounters))

- No per-step roll. A fight square is one p% chance per visit to the floor; wandering
  tokens (up to 16 a floor) fight you when you share their square.
- A fight square's group: tier 0, 1 or 2 by a 0-99 roll, 65/25/10 on most floors
  (57/29/14 in the Training Maze), then uniform within the tier.
- One NPC party token on most floors, picked among the parties allowed there; none
  wander on the Grounds or in the Shrine while a fixed meeting there is pending.
- Further groups join while rand(100) < the previous monster's companion chance, up to
  the floor's maximum (1 to 4). ([D-battle.md, "Encounter setup"](D-battle.md#encounter-setup))
- Surprise: 19% the party surprises the monsters; otherwise 19% of the rest (15.4%
  overall) the monsters surprise the party.

### Battle ([D-battle.md](D-battle.md))

- **Initiative**, lower first: party rand(10), minus (agility - 13) at 14+, plus
  (4 - agility/2) under 8, minus 2 more for a character with extra swings whose tick is
  above 2, at most 10; monsters (rand(255) & 7) + 2, so 2-9.
- **Party to hit**: T = base (20-23 for monster groups 1-4) - AC + AC modifier - to-hit,
  at most 19 (Oslo then lowers it), at least 1; each swing hits on rand(20) >= T.
- **Monster to hit**: T = 22 (+ Maporfic) - AC + AC modifier - (level/2 + 1), +4 if the
  target defends, 1-19.
- **Damage**: the weapon's dice; + rand(damage) against a target confused, poisoned or
  worse, or from hiding; × 2 for a slaying weapon that matches.
- **Spells**: resisted on rand(100) < resistance - Nofis cuts + 1 (so always at least 1%).
  Halito 2d4, Mahalito 4d6, Lahalito 6d6, Madalto 8d8, Ladalto 10d10, Tiltowait 10d15 on
  every group, Zilwan 255d10 on undead only. Alchemist spells are three-round clouds.
  ([Damage spells](D-battle.md#damage-spells))
- **Heals**: Dios 2-8, Dial 2-16, Dialma 3-24, Diomas 3-6, Madial 17-68 per member in
  battle (10-50 in camp, [B-camp.md](B-camp.md#the-26-camp-spells)).
- **Fleeing**: chance 80 if the monsters' total levels are higher, else 80 + 5 × (party
  levels / monster levels), minus 2 × the area's danger level; succeeds on
  rand(100) + 1 < chance. Fixed battles can't be fled.
- **Rewards**: experience and chest gold are split among members below status 0x20.

### Wipes and bodies ([A-maze.md §4-6](A-maze.md#4-when-the-party-is-wiped-out), [fallen-parties.md](fallen-parties.md))

- Lost members deleted; every member's gold halved; one member's items rolled six times
  (rand(12) < 8 destroys a rare item, < 3 any other), the others keep everything (a
  bug, [bugs.md 3.1](bugs.md)).
- Bodies stay on their square and nothing takes from them later. Look for Companions
  on that exact square brings them back free.
- NPC rescue fees ([C-talk.md §5](C-talk.md#5-services), played through in
  [rescue-test.md](rescue-test.md)): Guy all the gold; Balbo every item; Fontana half
  the gold and the items in the first, third, fifth, seventh and ninth slots. In the
  original a rescued party stayed a candidate, so it could be offered and charged again;
  fixed ([bugs.md 1.13](bugs.md)), the Yes frees its record.

### Locks and chests ([lock-test.md](lock-test.md), [D-battle.md](D-battle.md#chests-and-traps))

- **Picking a lock**: 70% if (level, + 5 for a Thief or Ninja, + 2 for a Ranger or
  Bard) × 5 + Luck, over 10, reaches the door's difficulty, else 15%; minus 10 for each
  failure (to 50), which wears off a step at a time. The level and Luck come from roster
  chosen member (until the fix, bugs.md 1.14, from roster entry 1-5 picked by the cursor's blink). No other
  cost. Calnova on a door: 95%, 0% on anti-magic squares.
- **Chests**: Inspect is right Agility × 6% for a Thief (× 5 Ninja, × 4 Ranger and
  Bard, × 1 others, 95% at most); a miss names a random trap, or sets it off if
  rand(20) > Agility. Calfo 95%. Disarm needs the right trap named (a wrong one sets it
  off): 80 + level - danger for a Thief or Ninja. Open always sets off a trap.

### Raising the dead

- **Temple** ([E-town.md](E-town.md#the-temple-temple-of-cant)): fee level × 100
  (paralyzed), × 200 (stoned), × 250 (dead), × 500 (ashes), paid before the roll. Dead:
  rand_upto(99) < 60 + 2 × VIT revives at 1 HP, else ashes. Ashes: < 68 + 2 × VIT
  revives at full HP, else Lost.
- **Kadorto in camp** ([B-camp.md](B-camp.md#kadorto)): VIT drops by 0 or 1; under 4 the
  dead become ashes and ashes are Lost; else rand_upto(80) < VIT × 4 succeeds, at full
  HP and 1-52 weeks older.
- **Kadorto from an NPC** ([C-talk.md §5](C-talk.md#5-services)): always works, free, no
  VIT loss or aging, on the dead and on ashes.
- **The Rod of Raising and the Book of Life**: no roll. ([B-camp.md](B-camp.md#use))

### Town ([E-town.md](E-town.md))

- **Inn**: 0 / 10 / 50 / 200 / 500 / 10,000 G a week for +0 / 1 / 3 / 7 / 10 HP / full;
  each paid week ages a week, the Stables a day; one level-up chance per stay.
- **Boltac's**: sells at half price (potions and scrolls a tenth per use left);
  identify and uncurse cost half the price, and uncursing destroys the item.
- **Level-up** ([G-records.md](G-records.md#level-up-stats-and-aging)): each stat is
  considered 76% of the time; if rand(0..59) < age in years it may drop (and under 4 VIT
  the character dies of old age, Lost); otherwise it rises when under the race base + 10
  and rand(0..41) > the stat.
- **HP per level** ([G-records.md](G-records.md#hit-points)): Fighter, Lord, Valkyrie
  4-10; Samurai 4-9; Priest, Ranger 3-8; Thief, Bard, Bishop, Ninja, Monk 3-6; Mage,
  Alchemist, Psionic 2-4; adjusted by VIT.

### Creating a character ([F-edge-save.md](F-edge-save.md#creating-a-character))

- Bonus points: 7-10 (20/21, about 95.2%), 17-20 (about 4.5%), 27-30 (about 0.23%).
- "Retrain?" (after accepting a character with under 10 points): three years older and
  three levels up, to level 4.
- Class change: each stat to the higher of the race base and the class minimum, three
  years older, everything not cursed unequipped.

### Items ([B-camp.md](B-camp.md#identify-bishops), [G-records.md](G-records.md#item-record-0x4c-bytes-382-at-0x80052bf2))

- A Bishop identifies on (level - item level + 1) × 20%, held to 5-75%.
- Equipping an item of the wrong alignment curses it; wrong class, race or sex refuses.
- The Gold armor's spells break the piece half the time; Matokani kills anything not
  undead of level 8 or below, with no resistance roll. ([D-battle.md](D-battle.md#items-in-battle))
