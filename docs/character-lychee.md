# Lychee: character profile (2026-11-05)

Written before her voice review, the way Linaria's and Lilia's were, to check whether
her English rests on a misreading. It's drawn from her 41 tagged strings, all in
NPC_MES1 (tags ライチ and フードをかぶった女), the three places others mention her
(1:95, 1:118 and the 1:99 tag), the talk-script dumps in `rips/re/work/C/` for who
speaks around her, her two portraits, and the sources at the end. Her lines, in
Japanese with the current English, are in `rips/review/lychee-lines.txt`, which isn't
committed, like the other review files.

## Short version

Lychee is the party's Mage, but her type isn't "little kid". She's the cutesy teenage
witch girl, a 魔女っ子, played as a ぶりっ子: a girl who acts cute on purpose. She calls
herself "Lychee" as an affectation, giggles (キャハッ!, えへ, エヘヘ), pouts (教えてあげない
モン), coos over a party of "cute boys" who gave her an item (Guy calls them "nothing but
kids"),
and makes things up and takes them back ("I can fly on a broom! ...Kidding, kidding!
Sorry"). She's also the party's soft-hearted airhead: she can't bring herself to kill a
cute rabbit, she says she didn't understand a word the scholar said, and she forgets
what she just told you. With strangers she drops all of that and speaks politely:
何かご用ですか?, あなた方のお連れさんではありませんか?

The current voice sheet calls her "the airheaded child-mage", and the current English
writes her as a small child with folksy spellings: "Nice to meetcha", "y'know" seven
times, "wanna", "s'posed". It also stretches words the Japanese doesn't ("Hellooo!",
"anythiiing", "reeeally"), and it puts her name where she says あたし. Her airheadedness
reads right. Her age, her register and how often she uses her name are wrong. The
misreading is the same size as Linaria's: the character holds, but the tone of the
words has to change.

## The trope, from the game

- **The Mage of a DQ3 party.** Alstron introduces her as 魔法使いのライチ (1:118), the
  Mage slot of Guy's Hero/Soldier/Priest/Mage lineup (`docs/npc-translation.md`, "Guy's
  party and Dragon Quest III"). Unlike Linaria, she isn't drawn as her DQ3 counterpart:
  the DQ3 female Mage wears a black pointed hat, a green robe and a brown cape, with
  short reddish hair (pixiv, DQ dictionary wiki). Lychee wears a red hooded robe trimmed
  in white, with long blonde braids, and in battle she holds up a star-tipped wand and
  kicks one heel up (`rips/review/lychee/`, enlarged from her face and her battle
  portrait). The look is the anime witch girl, not the Dragon Quest Mage.
- **Hooded.** Her tag before you know her name is フードをかぶった女, "the woman in the
  hood" (1:5, 1:9, 1:26, 1:99), and Alstron points her out as 頭巾をかぶっているの
  (1:118). The tag says 女, the same word as Linaria's 大柄な女, not 女の子 ("girl"). A
  red 頭巾 may be a nod to 赤ずきん (Little Red Riding Hood), but nothing in her text
  says so. Treat that as a guess, not a joke to translate.
- **Magical-girl jokes.** She's "good at transformation magic", 変身する魔法 (1:252).
  Wizardry has no transformation spell, and 変身 is the magical girl's signature word.
  She claims she can fly on a broom, then takes it back (1:253). She's into summoning
  lately (1:239), and boasts about summoning a Greater Demon (1:260). Every one of these
  belongs to the witch-girl picture, not the Wizardry Mage.
- **The ぶりっ子 laugh and pout.** キャハッ! (1:227), えへ (1:96), エヘヘ (1:139),
  へへー (1:198), and the pouting モン and もん (1:99, 1:186).

## How old?

The game never says. The evidence points to a teenager acting cute, not a small child:

- Her portrait is a teenage girl or young woman, the same age range as Lilia's.
- Her tag says 女, not 女の子 (1:5).
- She calls Fontana's party "cute boys" (かわいい男の子, 1:198). Guy says the same party
  was "nothing but kids" (1:195), and Linaria and Alstron call its leader little and young
  (1:196, 1:197). She's cooing over children, not crushing on boys, which puts her older
  than them. (The first version of this profile called her "boy-crazy" on this one line;
  in context it doesn't hold.)
- She speaks polite Japanese with strangers (1:22, 1:66, 1:68, 1:72) and says お久しぶり
  ね to old parties (1:70, 1:71). A little girl in fiction doesn't talk that way.
- She doesn't always use her name. She says あたし twice (1:137, 1:260) and 私 once
  (1:214). Her name is a cute habit she reaches for, not the only way she can talk.
- Using your own name as "I" is what small children do, but in anime it's also what
  cutesy girls and idols do, mostly girls, because it's cute. Kinsui's 役割語 is about
  exactly this kind of fiction shorthand. Japanese With Anime puts it the same way.

So "child" is the misread. Note that `docs/character-linaria.md` repeated it ("Lychee
is a child"). That line is corrected along with this profile.

## Personality, from what she says

- **Cutesy on purpose.** Her name for "I", the giggles, the doubled openers (あのね,
  あのね; ねぇねぇねぇ。聞いて,聞いて~), and the brag-and-fish lines (すごいっしょ,
  すごいっしょ~ 1:260; へへー,いいでしょー 1:198).
- **Soft-hearted.** She couldn't kill 可愛いベーナモちゃん, "the cute little Magical
  Rabbit", made friends with it, and that turned the whole party back to Good (1:95,
  1:96). She was so scared of the creepy stranger that she never wants to see him again
  (1:221).
- **Airhead and klutz.** She gave a pebble "a little kick" and the maze walls changed,
  and she insists it wasn't her (1:99, 1:102). She didn't understand a word Cleo said
  (1:214). She doesn't think there's any big news, "though I don't really know" (1:245).
  She forgot she'd already introduced herself (1:138, 1:139).
- **A tease and a fibber.** "I can fly on a broom! I can't carry two, though. Kidding,
  kidding! Sorry" (1:253). She's "not telling" when the others answer (1:186).
- **Loyal.** Zaril asked her to join his party, and she turned him down ちゃーんと,
  "properly", the way a good party member should (1:210).
- **Polite with strangers.** 何かご用ですか? (1:22, 1:66), the long polite question
  about the lonely drinker at the tavern (1:68), and the stiff, embarrassed distance of
  1:72 (あなた方とはあまりお話をしたくない。何と呼べば良いのですか?).

## What she doesn't use

- No rough or boyish forms: no オレ, ボク, ぜ, だろ, ちまう.
- No あんた or おまえ: she says あなた, あなた方 or {ff12}さん.
- No dialect and no dropped sounds. ゃ-contractions like わかんない and 信じらんない are
  ordinary casual speech, not slang. "Meetcha", "wanna", "s'posed" and "y'know" have no
  counterpart in her Japanese.
- No baby talk: no でちゅ, no lisping, nothing misspoken. Her cuteness is in the name,
  the giggles, the stretched endings and the particles.
- No stretching where the Japanese doesn't stretch. こんにちはっ! is clipped short
  (1:67), and 何も知らないよ (1:180), 凝ってるんだ (1:239) and バイバイ (1:26, 1:44)
  are said plainly.

## What it means for the English

Keep the airhead, the giggles, the brag-and-fish lines, the doubled openers and her
name where she uses it. Change the rest:

- **Use her name only where she does.** 1:260 says あたし, so "I summoned a Greater
  Demon!" 1:72 and 1:186 have no ライチ either. Thirteen lines with her name is plenty.
- **Drop the folksy spellings:** "meetcha", "wanna", "s'posed", "y'know". For あのね,
  "You know what?" or "Guess what?" works. For a trailing ね/ねぇ after her name
  (ライチねぇ, ...), use nothing or a comma, as in "Lychee's a Mage, you know~" only where
  the stretch is there.
- **Stretch only what the Japanese stretches.** A stretched sentence ending (なのー,
  ないよぉ~) goes on the last word of the English sentence: "Lychee's a Maaage!" (1:131),
  "nothing at aaall" (1:233). An earlier draft of this note said not "Maaage"; the scene
  follows the 1:233 rule instead.
- **Keep her polite lines polite** (1:22, 1:66, 1:68, 1:72), so the switch shows, as
  with Linaria.
- **Don't double what she says once.** 1:198 has それでね once, not "And then, and then".

## Lines to look at in review

Name or register:
- 1:260 "Lychee summoned a Greater Demon!" says あたし: "I summoned...".
- 1:72 "I don't really wanna talk to you guys. What's Lychee s'posed to call you?" The
  Japanese is polite and distant, with no ライチ.
- 1:186 "Lychee's not telling!" for 教えてあげないモン, with no ライチ: "I'm not telling~".
- 1:119 "Nice to meetcha!" for よろしくネ.
- 1:68 "Y'know, at the tavern..." adds the "Y'know", and the Japanese is polite.
- 1:96 "I didn't wanna kill...".
- 1:206 "Y'know, y'know" for あのね,あのね: "Guess what, guess what?".
- 1:198, 210, 214, 253 "y'know" for それでね / 地下でねぇ / あとね.
- 1:210 "just like she should!", where she talks about herself as "she".

Stretching added, or moved:
- 1:67 "Hellooo!" for こんにちはっ!, which is clipped.
- 1:180 "anythiiing", 1:239 "reeeally", 1:26 and 1:44 "Bye-byeee" for a plain バイバイ.
- 1:131 "Lychee's a Maaage, y'know!" for ライチねー,魔法使いなのー.
- 1:32 "Seeee ya!" for まったねー (the stretch is on the end).

Extra or awkward:
- 1:38 "Okay, see ya, bye-byeee, see you again!" reorders and adds "again". うん,
  それじゃ,バイバーイ,またね is "Okay, well then, bye-byeee, see you!".
- 1:198 "And then, and then" doubles a single それでね. "Jealous?" for いいでしょー is
  close, but the line is boasting more than taunting: "Lucky me, huh?"
- 1:214 "lots and lots" for いろいろ.
- 1:233 "Nothing's different" for 変わったこと: "Nothing new, nothing at aaall~".
- 1:277 "Wowww" for へぇー, which is "Ohhh" or "Huh~" (Linaria's へぇ is "Huh").

## Something to check later

Alstron introduces Fritillaria as こっちの小柄な男性 (1:120), "this small man". 男性 is
the adult word for a man, and his tag is "Short man". The voice sheet called him a kid.
His own profile, `docs/character-fritillaria.md`, finds he's a small grown man,
probably a Hobbit.
